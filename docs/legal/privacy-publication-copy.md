# DeepHaus Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

## Who we are

Dekki Inc operates DeepHaus, a flashcard and spaced-repetition learning service,
including its website, mobile applications, and connected AI tools. This policy
explains how we handle personal information through those services. Contact us
about privacy at **info@dekki.ai**.

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** email address, account identifiers, profile details you
  provide, sign-in information, and university-verification information if used.
- **Learning content:** documents, text, links, audio, video, images, imported
  decks, flashcards, tags, and other material you choose to provide or generate.
- **Study activity:** review answers or grades you submit, scheduling information,
  progress, settings, and usage of learning features.
- **Billing information:** plan, subscription status, purchase identifiers, and
  billing events received from payment providers. Payment providers collect the
  payment details needed for checkout under their own notices.
- **Technical information:** device and browser information, IP addresses, request
  and error logs, feature interactions, and information stored in cookies or local
  storage. When enabled, product analytics may include page views, interaction
  events, error reports, and session replay. Signed-in analytics can be associated
  with your account identifier, email address, and name.
- **Support and connections:** messages you send us, authorizations you grant to
  connected services, and credentials or tokens needed to maintain those connections.

## How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; store and synchronize learning
content; generate or edit study material when requested; schedule reviews; display
progress; administer subscriptions; provide support; diagnose problems; understand
and improve use of the service; prevent misuse; and meet applicable obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the service
under our agreement with you, legitimate interests in operating and securing the
service where permitted, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where
required. You may withdraw consent for optional processing without changing the
lawfulness of earlier processing.

## AI features and connected assistants

When you request an AI feature in DeepHaus, relevant content and instructions may
be sent to the providers used to perform that feature, such as OpenAI for language
or audio processing and Mistral for document extraction. Avoid submitting content
you are not authorized to share. AI-generated material may be inaccurate.

When you connect DeepHaus to ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, or another MCP client, you
authorize that host to access your DeepHaus account within the approved scopes:

- **Study access** reads decks, cards, and learning statistics and can record
  review grades that change your study schedule.
- **Write access** creates, edits, renames, or deletes supported learning content.

The host receives the results of its authorized tool calls. DeepHaus receives the
content and parameters the host submits to those tools, such as cards you ask it
to save. Connecting does not itself give DeepHaus access to your entire chat
history. A host may include conversation content in a tool request you authorize.
The host's own terms and privacy policy govern its handling of information.

You can revoke a connection in DeepHaus's MCP connections settings. Revocation
stops subsequent authorized access but does not remove data already held by a
host. Contact that host or use its controls to manage those copies.

## When information is shared

Information is processed by service providers that help us host, secure, deliver,
and support DeepHaus. Depending on the features used, these include Supabase for
authentication, database and storage; Vercel and Render for hosting and processing;
PowerSync for synchronization; OpenAI and Mistral for requested AI features;
RevenueCat, Stripe, and applicable app stores for subscriptions; Resend for email;
and PostHog for analytics and diagnostics.

We also share information with integrations you choose to connect and make
content available to others when you explicitly publish or share it. Information
may be disclosed where legally required, to protect rights and security, or in
connection with a business transaction subject to applicable protections.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

We retain account and learning information to provide the service while your
account remains active. You can delete supported content and request account
deletion through the service or contact info@dekki.ai. Deletion may require
processing time and verification of your identity. Some records may be retained
where needed for legal, billing, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.
Backup and operational copies may persist until removed under the applicable
retention schedule.

Downloaded or cached study material can remain on your devices. Copies already
exported, shared, or received by another service are managed separately from your
DeepHaus account. Uninstalling an application does not by itself delete a cloud
account or cancel an app-store subscription.

Our providers may process information outside your province, state, or country,
including in the United States. Contact us for information about the transfer arrangements and protections
that apply to your account.

## Security and your choices

We use access controls and other technical and organizational measures to protect
information. MCP authorization codes and tokens are stored as hashes. No service
can guarantee absolute security; protect your account and revoke connections you
no longer use.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or
obtain a copy of personal information; withdraw consent; object to or restrict
processing; and complain to a privacy regulator. Contact info@dekki.ai to make a
request. We may verify your identity and will respond according to applicable
law. We do not charge a fee or restrict service for exercising a right except as
permitted by law.

Browser controls can manage cookies and local storage, although disabling
essential storage may prevent sign-in or offline features from working. Contact
us about optional analytics choices. You can contact info@dekki.ai to withdraw consent for optional processing
or request assistance with your privacy choices.

## Children

DeepHaus accounts are intended for users aged 13 and older, subject to the
additional eligibility requirements in our Terms and applicable local law. We do
not currently offer accounts for children under 13. If you
believe a child has provided personal information without an appropriate basis,
contact info@dekki.ai so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## Changes and contact

We will update this policy as the service changes and identify the effective date
of each published version. Where required, we will provide additional notice or
seek consent for material changes. Privacy questions and requests should be sent
to **Dekki Inc — info@dekki.ai**.
